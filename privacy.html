Privacy policy

This policy describes the TikTok connection in LAIKA Studio Analytics, operated by Laika Tells.

Data and purpose

When you authorize the connection, TikTok provides account identifiers and credentials needed to maintain access. For linked public videos, Studio reads video identifiers, publication time, and counts of views, likes, comments and shares. Studio uses the counts to track a video’s performance over time. It requests the number of comments and does not request their text.

Storage and retention

Studio encrypts stored credentials with Windows Data Protection and saves analytics on the computer running Studio. TikTok API snapshots expire 29 days after collection. Studio removes expired snapshots while it is running. Manually entered figures and any files the creator attaches stay with the Topic until the creator removes the local records. Studio sends authorization and data requests to TikTok to operate the connection. The public information pages do not receive Studio credentials or analytics.

Disconnect or request deletion

Open Analytics in Studio and choose Disconnect TikTok to revoke the app’s access and clear its saved TikTok API snapshots. You can also revoke access in your TikTok account. For questions or requests to access or delete data, email laikatells@gmail.com.
